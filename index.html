Founder

Ujjaval Shah

Founder, JMD Enterprises

"We source and supply, all over the world, right time, every time."

Ujjaval started in banking. After an MBA in Finance from the Institute of Management Studies, Indore, he spent four years at HDFC Bank as Cluster Head for North Gujarat, leading a team of seven and building working-capital relationships with the region's businesses.

In 2014 he moved into trade, leading new business at Padamshree Globe TradeLinks, where he built a start-up model around global trade. Two years later, in November 2016, he founded JMD Enterprises in Vadodara to do one thing well: get India's best-known consumer brands onto shelves abroad.

Today JMD ships Haldiram's dry and frozen ranges, MTR, ITC, Parle, Gits, Kingfisher and spirits including Royal Challenge, Old Monk, Antiquity and Black Dog.
